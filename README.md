## BaitWatch

[BaitWatch](https://github.com/sreekarseera/baitwatch) is a browser extension that
flags likely scam and social-engineering signals in messages and web pages.
Detection runs locally by default.

The repo has reproducible benchmarks, regression tests, and a written list of
where it fails — I'd rather show the real limitations than an accuracy number
nobody can check.

---

## Kindred Hands

Kindred Hands is an e-commerce platform I'm building with my uncle to help
Indian weavers and handicraft producers sell directly to customers, without a
commission cut. I've worked on the platform itself, the multi-vendor
marketplace logic, evaluating a virtual try-on model (accuracy, latency, cost,
where it breaks), and automated smoke/regression testing.

It's private for now while I clean up the technical write-up. No customer or
artisan data will ever be published.

---

## Solar

I helped with procurement analysis and documentation review for a 6 MW rooftop
solar project in my family's business — comparing module and inverter specs,
vendor quotes, and warranty/degradation terms. I didn't design, certify, or
commission the plant myself. Any public write-up will use only redacted or
synthetic numbers.

---

## LoomLoop

Still at the requirements stage — no working prototype yet, so I'm not going to
pretend there is one. The idea is a low-voltage sensor rig (load cells,
motor/encoder feedback, vibration sensing) that could catch yarn-tension
problems and loom stoppages early, without taking any control away from the
weaver. I'll post schematics and real results once there's something to show.
